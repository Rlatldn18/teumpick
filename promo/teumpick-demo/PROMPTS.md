# AI 이미지 제작 프롬프트

모든 이미지는 Codex 내장 `image_gen` 도구로 만들었습니다. 아래 내용은 생성에 사용한 최종 프롬프트 세트의 핵심 지시문입니다.

1. **`locker.png` — 기준 보관함:** photorealistic studio product photograph; freestanding off-white powder-coated cabinet with forest-green accents, exactly 12 equal compartments in a 3-column by 4-row grid, central numeric keypad and display on a slim side strip, all doors closed, no people, no text/logo, transparent background. 영상 조립 단계에서 틈픽 간판과 01–12 번호를 동일 위치에 추가.
2. **`commuter-phone.png` — 이용자:** relatable Korean commuter woman in her late 20s, sage jacket, dark pants, white sneakers, canvas bag, holding a blank-screen smartphone, full-body photoreal editorial cutout, transparent background.
3. **`station.png` — 배경:** clean modern Seoul metro transfer concourse, neutral pale stone walls, warm linear lights, subtle green wayfinding accents, open wall and floor area for a fixed cabinet, no locker, people, logo or readable text.
4. **`seller-prep.png` — 가게:** Korean male café owner in his early 30s wearing a dark-green apron, placing a fresh meal and drink into a cream paper takeout bag at a tidy counter, natural warm editorial photo, no logo/text.
5. **`commuter-keypad.png` — 코드 입력:** preserve the exact commuter identity and outfit from `commuter-phone.png`; change only to a three-quarter pose reaching toward a keypad off-frame while holding a smartphone; transparent background, no locker.
6. **`commuter-bag.png` — 수령 후:** preserve the exact commuter identity and outfit from `commuter-phone.png`; change only to a walking pose holding a cream takeout bag and phone; transparent background, no locker.
7. **`salad.png` — 메뉴 사진:** photorealistic overhead grilled chicken salad in a round bowl with greens, avocado, tomatoes, purple cabbage and chickpeas on a light wood table; no text or logo.

새로운 보관함 이미지를 샷마다 생성하지 않았습니다. 실물 장치처럼 보이는 여러 컷은 같은 `locker.png` 자산을 재사용하고 위치·크기만 바꾼 것입니다.
